Privacy Policy

Effective 23 November 2026

The short version

Flap Day does not collect, store or share any of your personal data. Your calendar stays on your devices. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

Your calendar

If you allow it, Flap Day reads and writes events through Apple's calendar system on your device, so it can show your day on the board and save events you add. This happens entirely on your iPhone, iPad or Apple Watch. Flap Day never sends your events, calendars or any other information to us or to anyone else.

You can turn calendar access off at any time in the Settings app under Apps › Flap Day › Calendars. Until you allow access, Flap Day shows a made-up sample day.

Widgets, Live Activity and Apple Watch

The widgets, the Live Activity and the Apple Watch app read your events on the device they run on, in the same way as the app. Nothing leaves your devices.

Your settings

Your preferences, such as appearance, flap speed, sound and which calendars are hidden, are saved on your device only. Deleting Flap Day removes them.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect or receive any data about you or how you use the app.
  • We don't use analytics, advertising or tracking, and don't include any third-party SDKs that do.
  • We don't sell or share data, because we don't have any.
  • Flap Day doesn't connect to any server we run.

Children

Flap Day collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
