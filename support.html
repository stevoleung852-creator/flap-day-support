Getting started

Flap Day opens on a sample day so you can try the board straight away. When you're ready, tap Use my calendar at the top of the board, or choose My calendar in Settings, and allow calendar access.

Flap Day shows the calendars already in Apple's Calendar app, including iCloud, Google and Exchange accounts you've added there. Events you add in Flap Day are saved to the Calendar app too.

Common questions

My events don't appear

Check that Flap Day has full calendar access: open the iPhone Settings app, go to Apps › Flap Day › Calendars and choose Full Access. Then make sure Settings in Flap Day is set to My calendar, not the sample day.

One calendar is missing from the board

Tapping a coloured circle at the top of the board hides that calendar. Tap it again, or turn it back on under Calendars on the board in Settings.

The widgets look out of date

Widgets refresh when an event starts or ends, about once an hour, and whenever you leave the app. Opening Flap Day brings them up to date.

The Live Activity doesn't show

It appears when your next event today is within four hours. Make sure Show next event is on in Flap Day's Settings, and that Live Activities are allowed for Flap Day in the iPhone Settings app. It updates when you open Flap Day.

How do I turn off the flap sound?

In Flap Day, open Settings and turn off Flap sound or Haptics. The sound also follows your iPhone's silent mode. With Reduce Motion on, the flaps don't animate.

My Apple Watch shows the sample day

Calendar access on Apple Watch is separate from your iPhone. Open Flap Day on the watch, scroll to the bottom and tap Use my calendar. The watch app shows your events but can't add or change them.

What do I need?

iPhone or iPad with iOS 18 or iPadOS 18 or later, and for the watch app, Apple Watch with watchOS 11 or later.
