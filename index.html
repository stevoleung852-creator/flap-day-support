Your day on a departure board.

Flap Day turns your calendar into a split-flap board. Every event flips into place with a soft click and counts down to what's next, on iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch.

Coming soon to the App Store

What it does

  • A board for your dayEach event's time, place and a countdown to when it starts, in flaps you can hear and feel.
  • Works with Apple CalendarShows the calendars on your iPhone, and events you add appear in Calendar too.
  • Widgets and Live ActivityThree Home Screen sizes, Lock Screen widgets, and a countdown in the Dynamic Island.
  • On Apple WatchYour remaining events on your wrist, plus a watch face complication.
  • Light or darkFollows your device, or pick one. Choose the flap speed, sound and haptics.
  • Private by designNo account, no tracking. Your calendar stays on your devices.
